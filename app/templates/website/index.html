Important Wallet Safety Instructions

Don't use .APK

Business Wallet open hone ke turant baad RushPay, MovPay ya kisi bhi third-party payment APK/App me login na karein.

✅ Wallet activate hone ke baad kam se kam 7–8 din tak sirf official Play Store se related app install karke normal aur limited use karein.

✅ 7–8 din complete hone ke baad hi kisi other 3rd Party platform/app me wallet use karna start karein.

🚫 Har Wallet me ak bot hota hai jo shuruwati dino me apke wallet gatividhiyo pe Nazar rkhta hai Jaldi login/use karne ki wajah se agar wallet Blocked, Restricted ya Disable hota hai, to uski responsibility India Business Wallets ki nahi hogi.

🔐 Apne wallet ko safe rakhne ke liye in instructions ko strictly follow karein.
